    Questions people ask
    How long does gel last?

    Usually 2 to 3 weeks, depending on how hard you are on your hands. BIAB tends to last 3 to 4 weeks because it's thicker.

    Do you remove gel from other studios?

    Yes. Removing another studio's gel or extensions is ₹400 and takes about 25 minutes. Add it when you book so we leave enough time.

    What does "10-free" mean?

    The polish is made without the ten ingredients most often flagged in nail products, such as formaldehyde, toluene and DBP.

    Can I bring my own design?

    Please do. Send a picture on WhatsApp and we'll reply with a price and how long it takes before you come in.

    What if a nail chips?

    If anything chips or lifts within 5 days, come back and we'll fix it for free.

    Do you take walk-ins?

    When there's a free chair, yes. Weekends fill up fast, so booking is safer.
